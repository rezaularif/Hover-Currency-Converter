# Chrome Web Store listing — Hover Currency Converter

## Name
Hover Currency Converter

## Package summary
Convert currency prices on hover - see converted values instantly when hovering over prices

## Detailed description
Understand international prices without leaving the page.

Hover Currency Converter shows a price in your preferred currency when you hover over it. Compare products, check travel costs, or read prices in articles without copying amounts into another tab.

Convert as you browse
• Hover over common price formats such as $19.99, €10,39, R$ 19,99, 1.299,00 kr., or 19.99 USD.
• Shared symbols such as kr, $ and ¥ are matched to the right currency using the page's product data, address, and language. Local prices in your own currency are left alone.
• Tell the extension which currency a site uses, or choose a default for each shared symbol.
• Search for your target currency, and your Quick Convert currency, by code or name in the side panel.
• Enter an amount in the quick converter and swap the source and target currencies.
• Choose decimal places, tooltip position, and appearance.
• Turn conversions off globally or for a site.
• Use keyboard focus on supported elements, Enter to toggle the conversion, and Escape to dismiss it.

Get started
1. Install the extension and open it from Chrome’s Extensions menu.
2. Choose your target currency in the side panel.
3. Visit a page with prices and hover over an amount.

Privacy and storage
Price detection and conversion happen in your browser. Page text and prices are not sent to the currency APIs. The extension downloads public exchange rates and currency names, with no tracking or analytics.

Currency choices, enable settings, the disabled site list, and currency detection settings use Chrome sync storage and may sync across your signed-in browsers. Appearance preferences and cached rates stay on your device.

About conversions
An internet connection is needed to download fresh rates. Cached rates may remain available offline and can be out of date. Conversions are estimates, not bank or card quotes, and do not include transaction fees.

Detection depends on how a website displays its prices. Some formats and restricted Chrome pages are not supported. If a shared symbol is matched to the wrong currency on a site, set that site's currency in the side panel under Currency Detection.

What’s new in 1.1.1
• Shared currency symbols (kr, $, £, ¥, Rs, ﷼, 元, C$, B$) are now detected from the page instead of always being treated as one currency. For example, Danish prices in kr are no longer read as Swedish kronor.
• New Currency Detection settings: choose the currency a site uses, or a default for each shared symbol.
• The Quick Convert currency is now searchable, just like Target Currency.
• Supports more formats, including kr. 1.299,00, Rs. 1,499, CN¥, JP¥, 円 and 元.
• Much lighter hovering: less work on busy pages, fewer accidental tooltips, and faster results.
• No new permissions or tracking.
